What's new · August 2026

Anyone can run a game now

Four things changed on the board this week. The short version: you no longer need the organizer to post a game, the skill picker finally has an honest middle, and finished games stop piling up.

If you just turn up and play

two changes

Five skill levels, not three

Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced gave most people nothing honest to pick. Nobody wants to call themselves the weakest player in the gym, and nobody wants to over-claim either — so almost everyone chose Intermediate, and the teams came out lopsided anyway.

Two new rungs in the gaps where everyone was already standing

Your existing sign-ups didn't change. If you picked Intermediate before, you're still Intermediate — it just sits at 3 on a scale of 5 now, so the team mixer has something real to work with.

Finished games clear themselves

A game now disappears from the board an hour after it ends, taking its roster and its rounds with it. No more scrolling past three weeks of Fridays to find this one.

Games with no date set stay put until someone removes them.

If you're the one organizing

two changes

Post your own game, set your own passcode

You don't need the shared organizer passcode any more. Hit Post a game, fill in the details, and choose a passcode for that game while you're there.

That passcode is yours: it edits the game, mixes the teams, removes players and closes the sign-ups — on your phone, your laptop, anywhere. It does nothing to anyone else's game. Share it with whoever's helping you run the night.

Opening a game someone else posted? Tap I'm running this game and enter their passcode.

Fix the details after you've posted

Court moved, start pushed back, someone dropped out and you want a smaller cap — all editable now, under Edit game details. The name, date, start time, length, place, player cap and description are all fair game.

Rounds you've already mixed stay exactly as they were. They're a record of what got played, so editing the game doesn't rewrite them. Changing the date or time also moves when the game clears itself off the board.

One thing that already happened

Both Friday Volleyball games from 21 August had already finished when the cleanup went live, so they were removed along with their rosters. The board is empty and ready for the next one — post it whenever you like.
