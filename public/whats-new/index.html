The board keeps score now

Until now the board recorded who played with whom and stopped there. It now also records who won — and puts up the night's standings when you're done. Everything else works exactly as it did.

New this month

two changes

Tap the side that won

Every mix now lists its matchups underneath the teams. Two sides is one matchup. Four sides is all six pairings, because with several sides up you play each other side in turn rather than all at once.

Anyone can tap it in — you don't need to be the one running the game. Whoever's nearest the phone does it.

Nothing has to be filled in. A pairing you never got around to playing just stays blank, and blank pairings count for nothing — the night above is recorded as three results, not as three losses. Tapping a side that's already marked as the winner clears it, which is the quickest way out of a mis-tap.

Standings when the night's over

Once any result is in, the session grows a Tonight board: everyone who played, their record, and 🥇 🥈 🥉 across the top three. It counts every round of the session, not just the last one.
